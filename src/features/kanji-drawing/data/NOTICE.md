# Kanji handwriting template attribution

The stroke geometry in `templates.ts` is adapted from KanjiVG by Ulrich Apel and its contributors. The sampled geometry is licensed under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). [KanjiVG r20260714](https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg/tree/r20260714)

The templates cover the characters in the JLPT Trainer N5–N3 kanji workbooks. Each row stores the character, stroke count, and Base64-encoded coordinate pairs. Each stroke has eight points sampled at equal arc-length intervals. Coordinates are centered and scaled for the whole character and quantized to bytes.
